About xGuess

xGuess is a web application which is a part of quix, used to guess the football players from 2-3 clues. (this is a web application made for having fun, and especially for football fans to test their ball knowledge yk). Also you need to name the player's FULL EXACT NAME, it should be same as in our data else you are gonna be wrong in the charge of speeling error.
